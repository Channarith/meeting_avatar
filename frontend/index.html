Show up in the meeting

You join from your own account. The room sees this headshot as your camera and hears the avatar as your microphone. The full-body model is not used.

  1. Click Pop out camera. That window is the live avatar. OBS is not installed on this Mac, so this page cannot publish a virtual camera itself. Install OBS, window-capture the popped-out page, and start Virtual Camera.
  2. Install BlackHole 2ch and BlackHole 16ch. In Audio MIDI Setup, make a Multi-Output device called Meeting Listen from your headphones plus BlackHole 16ch, and another called Avatar Voice from your headphones plus BlackHole 2ch.
  3. In Zoom, set the camera to the device named OBS Virtual Camera, the microphone to BlackHole 2ch, and the speaker to Meeting Listen.
  4. Set this Mac’s output, or this page’s speak device, to Avatar Voice. Set Listen microphone to BlackHole 16ch.
  5. Those two BlackHole devices have to stay different, or the avatar will hear itself.
  6. Open the meeting link and go live. The introduction says this is your avatar. If someone asks, it will say so again.

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack huddles all pick the camera and microphone in their device settings. Speech uses the male xAI voice, then a male Mac voice if xAI cannot be reached. The same app is also packaged in Docker on port 8081.
